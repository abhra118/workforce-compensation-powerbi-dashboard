## Project Overview

This dashboard helps HR teams and business managers monitor workforce composition, compensation trends, employee tenure, and department-wise performance through interactive Power BI visualizations.

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 – Executive Overview

* Total Employees
* Total Gross Pay
* Total Net Pay
* Total Deductions
* Average Gross Pay
* Average Tenure
* Employees by Department
* Workforce by Gender
* Workforce by Age Group
* Workforce by Tenure

### Page 2 – Employee Compensation & Salary Analysis

* Gross vs Net Pay by Department
* Gross Pay by Gender
* Deductions by Department
* Top 10 Designations by Average Gross Pay
* Average Pay & Workforce by Tenure

### Page 3 – Workforce Analysis

* Employee Joining Trend
* Top 10 Designations by Workforce
* Top 10 Departments by Workforce
* Employee Details Table
